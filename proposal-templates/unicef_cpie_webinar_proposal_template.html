02 / Understanding of the Assignment

Understanding of the Assignment

UNICEF seeks to engage a qualified institutional contractor to design and deliver an engaging, practical and interactive online webinar on the Return to Happiness approach and Trauma-Informed Child Protection in Emergencies for practitioners across the Eastern Caribbean.

The assignment is grounded in the reality that children affected by emergencies are often exposed to trauma, displacement, violence and the disruption of essential support systems. These experiences carry long-term effects on psychosocial well-being and development, so child protection interventions must go beyond basic service delivery and incorporate trauma-informed, child-centred approaches that promote safety, recovery and resilience.

Online webinarDelivered live and interactively
Eastern CaribbeanRegionally contextualised content
RTH and CPiETwo frameworks, one curriculum
Pre and postMeasured learning outcomes
The training must be practical and application-oriented so that participants can translate what they learn into interventions inside their own contexts, aligned with global CPiE standards.

What the consultancy strengthens

Understanding of how trauma affects children in emergencies; the trauma-informed principles of safety, trust, empowerment and cultural sensitivity; the Return to Happiness approach as a structured psychosocial intervention; and the integration of all three into everyday CPiE programming.

What the contractor delivers

A contextually relevant curriculum, a complete training package of presentations, facilitator notes, participant materials and case studies, live participatory sessions, pre- and post-training assessment, technical support throughout, and a final report on outcomes and engagement.

04 / Training Approach and Methodology

Training Approach and Methodology

We adopt a structured, participatory and results-oriented approach to designing and delivering the webinar, built to produce practical learning, strong engagement and measurable outcomes.

Phase 1 — Inception and planning

Review of the terms of reference and the relevant frameworks, clarification of target audience needs, and an inception report setting out objectives, methodology, session structure and timeline.

Phase 2 — Curriculum and package

A practical, user-friendly training package: presentations, a facilitator guide, participant materials and job aids, and case studies drawn from real scenarios.

Phase 3 — Interactive delivery

Live presentations and demonstrations, breakout discussions and group work, scenario-based exercises and real-time question and answer, so that participation is active rather than passive.

Phase 4 — Assessment

Pre- and post-training assessment together with engagement monitoring, providing clear evidence of what was learned rather than an assertion that learning occurred.

Phase 5 — Reporting. A final report covering session summaries and key outcomes, participation and engagement analysis, and feedback with recommendations.
