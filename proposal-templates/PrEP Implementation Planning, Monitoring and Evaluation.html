2. Understanding of the Assignment

Vantage Africa School of Leadership understands that Avenir Health is seeking a qualified sub-Saharan African organization to serve as a regional technical assistance partner for the PrEP Implementation Planning, Monitoring and Evaluation Tool (PrEP-it).

The assignment is focused on strengthening regional capacity for the effective use of PrEP-it in PrEP target setting, planning, costing, monitoring, evaluation, commodity forecasting, and data-informed decision-making. It will require participation in advanced training, contribution to updated training materials, co-facilitation of virtual and in-country technical assistance, and provision of follow-up support to users.

What Avenir Health Is Seeking

A qualified African organization with M&E capacity, training expertise, and regional reach to support PrEP-it technical assistance and long-term sustainability.

What This Assignment Must Achieve

Strengthen regional capacity for effective PrEP-it use; revise training materials; co-facilitate virtual sessions; provide in-country TA; build long-term African-led sustainability.

Who Should Benefit

Country teams, programme managers, MOH officials, and health data users responsible for PrEP planning, costing, target setting and commodity forecasting.

What Success Looks Like

Country users can independently apply PrEP-it for planning, forecasting, monitoring and reporting; and a sustainable African TA ecosystem is in place beyond the contract.

We further understand that the purpose of this consultancy is not only to deliver short-term support, but also to build sustainable African-led technical capacity. Vantage Africa School of Leadership is therefore well positioned to support this assignment through its expertise in monitoring and evaluation, quantitative analysis, training, technical assistance, and capacity strengthening across development and public health-related programmes.

4. Training Approach and Methodology

Vantage Africa School of Leadership will adopt a practical, participatory, and results-oriented approach designed to strengthen the technical capacity of participants in effectively using the PrEP-it tool for planning, monitoring, evaluation, forecasting, and decision-making.

Interactive Virtual Training Sessions

The training will be delivered through structured and interactive virtual sessions that encourage active participation, engagement, and knowledge sharing. Participants will be guided through the functionality, application, and practical use of the PrEP-it tool in different implementation contexts.

Hands-On Practical Demonstrations

Our approach will emphasize practical learning through live demonstrations and guided exercises using real-life scenarios and country-specific data examples. This will help participants gain confidence in applying the tool for PrEP planning, target setting, commodity forecasting, monitoring, and reporting.

Technical Coaching and Mentorship

Participants will receive continuous technical guidance and mentorship throughout the engagement period. This approach will support deeper understanding of the tool, strengthen analytical skills, and improve the ability of users to independently apply PrEP-it in their respective programmes and institutions.

Collaborative Learning and Problem Solving

The methodology will promote collaboration between participants, technical teams, and stakeholders through discussions, practical assignments, and shared learning experiences. This will support peer learning, strengthen regional technical networks, and encourage exchange of implementation experiences and best practices.

Continuous Technical Support and Feedback

Following the training sessions, Vantage Africa School of Leadership will provide ongoing technical assistance, troubleshooting support, and user feedback mechanisms to ensure effective application of the tool and timely resolution of technical challenges.

Sustainability and Capacity Strengthening

Our approach is designed not only to support immediate training needs but also to strengthen long-term regional technical capacity and sustainability. The methodology will therefore focus on knowledge transfer, institutional strengthening, and empowering local technical teams to continue supporting PrEP-it implementation beyond the consultancy period.
